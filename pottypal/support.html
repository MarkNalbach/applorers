Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I find every restroom near me?

Potty Pal relies on community-submitted locations. Our restroom database grows as users add and review locations.

How do I add a restroom?

Use the Add Restroom feature within the app to submit a new location for the community.

How do I unlock mini-games?

Mini-games are unlocked by contributing reviews and helping expand the Potty Pal community database.

Can I edit or remove my review?

If review editing is supported in your current version, you can manage your reviews from your profile or review history section.

How do I report a review or restroom listing?

If you believe a review or restroom listing contains inappropriate, misleading, offensive, or inaccurate content, please email us at applorers@gmail.com with details about the content so we can investigate.

Report Inappropriate Content

Potty Pal is a community-driven platform. If you encounter content that is inaccurate, inappropriate, offensive, abusive, or otherwise violates our community guidelines, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
