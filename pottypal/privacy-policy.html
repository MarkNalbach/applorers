Privacy Policy for Potty Pal

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

Potty Pal ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Potty Pal mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

Potty Pal requires users to create an account. Account creation and email verification are provided through Firebase Authentication. Email addresses are used for authentication and account verification purposes.

Location Information

Potty Pal may request access to your device's location while you are using the app. Location access is optional and is used to help you discover nearby restroom locations. You may enable or disable location permissions at any time through your device settings.

User-Generated Content

Potty Pal allows users to contribute content to the community, including:

  • Restroom ratings
  • Restroom reviews
  • User-submitted restroom locations
  • Other content voluntarily submitted through the app

Content submitted by users may be visible to other users of the application.

Usage Information

We may collect information about how users interact with the application in order to maintain functionality, improve performance, and enhance the user experience.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain Potty Pal services
  • Authenticate user accounts
  • Display restroom locations, reviews, and ratings
  • Allow users to contribute content to the community
  • Improve app performance and functionality
  • Monitor and moderate community content
  • Respond to support requests
  • Protect the security and integrity of the platform

Third-Party Services

Potty Pal uses third-party services to provide certain features and functionality. These services may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Firebase Authentication for account creation and email verification
  • Firebase services used to support app functionality and security
  • Mapping services used to display restroom locations and map data

User-Generated Content

Potty Pal is a community-driven platform. Reviews, ratings, restroom submissions, and other user-generated content may be visible to other users. Users are responsible for the content they submit and should avoid posting personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

Content Moderation

We reserve the right to review, moderate, edit, restrict, or remove user-generated content that violates our policies or contains spam, harassment, offensive material, misleading information, or other prohibited content.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect our rights, investigate misuse of the service, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

Users may choose whether to grant location permissions. Location access can be modified or revoked at any time through device settings.

Account Deletion

If you would like your account deleted, please contact us at the email address below. We will make reasonable efforts to remove your account and associated information, subject to any legal or operational requirements.

Children's Privacy

Potty Pal is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
